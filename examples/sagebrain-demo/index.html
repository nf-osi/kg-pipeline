Patient JH-2-002: genotype to measured drug response

setup

One engine, three graphs

Every query in this demo runs live against sagebrain’s read-only query API. The three graphs were built by different pipelines and have never been joined until now — the joins below are pure IRI equality, no crosswalks.

Framing rule, non-negotiable: n = 1, and say so. Specimen-level overlap between screening and variant data is 0/553; individual-level overlap is exactly this patient. This is a story about what the graph can hold: it already holds the experiment you would have proposed next.
hop 1 · genotype

The textbook NF1 → PRC2 transformation, in one patient

Four lesions, one exact VRS allele. NF1 p.F2083Pfs*15 is present in every lesion sequenced; SUZ12 p.Q424* appears only in the two that transformed to MPNST. Traversal, not manual variant matching.

hop 2 · the derived model & screening

Their own cells, already screened

A cell line derived from this patient’s tumour hangs off the same individual node. Screening files carry ChEMBL IRIs as of the v273 build — the free-text labels (212= trametinib) survive beside the resolved molecule.

hop 3 · mechanism → pathway · three graphs

No drug reaches NF1. The pathway is the route.

NF1 is not druggable — no mechanism edge lands on it anywhere in Open Targets. What the screened compounds reach is MAP2K1/MAP2K2, downstream of NF1, and Reactome supplies the connection: shared membership in the RAF/MAP kinase cascade. One query, three graphs.

hop 4 · regulatory status

The drug class is approved — in this exact disease

Joined on the patient’s own two tumour classes (EFO_0000658 plexiform neurofibroma, MONDO_0017827 MPNST). A stage belongs to a drug–disease pair, never to a drug — this chart never shows one without the other.

the loop, closed

The graph already held the experiment you’d propose next

  • Genotype. NF1 p.F2083Pfs*15 in all four lesions; SUZ12 p.Q424* only in the MPNSTs — one VRS allele, tracked by traversal.
  • Measured response. The patient’s own cell line, screened against trametinib, TNO155, ribociclib and the TNO155+ribociclib combination, with RNA-seq readout.
  • Mechanism. Trametinib, selumetinib and mirdametinib hit MAP2K1/2 — in NF1’s own RAF/MAP kinase cascade. Ribociclib’s CDK4/6 shares no NF1 pathway: its rationale is the combination, and the graph says so honestly.
  • Regulatory status. Two MEK inhibitors at APPROVAL for plexiform neurofibroma; trametinib — the one actually screened here — at PHASE_2.
Caveats, stated on stage: n = 1 — one patient, one derived model. Approval facts are per indication and population, never treatment guidance. Most compound free-text in the portal still does not resolve; ambiguity was refused, not guessed.
