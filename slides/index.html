Two Parallel Efforts to Improve Tools Portal Experience

Deploy integrated search and tool ecosystem with AI enhancement (GFF Addendum 7 — Year 1)

OpenSearch

Deliverable 1.1.1

Transition to improved OpenSearch infrastructure for faster, more relevant keyword and faceted search.

  • Better ranking & relevance
  • Faceted filtering improvements
  • Faster query performance

Scope: Keyword matching, filtering, and sorting over existing table data

AI Portal Assistant

Deliverable 1.1.2

An AI agent backed by a knowledge graph for natural-language question answering over portal data.

  • Cross-entity reasoning (joins, hops)
  • Additional data integration
  • Aggregation & comparison queries
  • Natural-language interface

Scope: Semantic retrieval, multi-hop reasoning, and conversational QA — queries that search cannot answer

These efforts are complementary. Better search improves everyday discovery, but some questions are categorically different — they require an AI agent that can traverse a knowledge graph, not just match keywords.

Why a Knowledge Graph for RAG?

What Search Will Do

  • Find studies by keyword
  • Filter files by data type
  • Sort results by date or relevance

What Only a KG + Agent Will Do

  • “Which cell lines carry NF1 mutations
    and have associated RNA-seq data?”
  • “What genetic reagents target genes
    studied in schwannoma models?”
  • “Compare mutation coverage across
    animal model organisms”
  • Multi-hop joins, aggregation, reasoning
Example evaluation questions Very High Frustration Advanced
CR-002 “Find human cell lines with the most diverse data types available on the portal” 2-hop CR-001 “Which cell lines have shown sensitivity to HDAC inhibitors?” 1-hop MUT-006 “Which mutations are available in both animal models and cell lines?” 2-hop

These require cross-resource aggregation, observation integration, or multi-table joins — impossible with keyword search or faceted filtering.
