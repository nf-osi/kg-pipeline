PMC7952412 · DISCUSS · Passage 40
The nerve-associated tumor from which we derived 2XSB cells was diagnosed as an MPNST after eliminating other diagnostic possibilities and its identification as an MPNST was supported by histologic, immunohistochemical and genomic analyses. The patient from which the tumor was resected had no previous diagnosis of any malignancy, lacked the clinical features characteristic of NF1 and had no previous history of radiotherapy, leading us to conclude that this tumor was a sporadic MPNST. High-density SNP array and whole exome sequencing analyses showed no evidence of inactivating NF1 mutations in either 2XSB cells or their parent tumor and we demonstrated that 2XSB cells expressed functional full-length neurofibromin protein, indicating that 2XSB cells are derived from the subset of sporadic MPNSTs that have intact NF1. As the pathogenesis of NF1-intact sporadic MPNSTs is poorly understood, the 2XSB cell line represents a valuable tool for investigating the mechanisms underlying the pathogenesis of neurofibromin-expressing sporadic MPNSTs. However, the distinct subpopulations of Sox2-positive cells and polyploid cells that we identified raise important questions about the biology of sporadic MPNSTs and their response to therapeutic agents. Although we recognize the risks of generalizing findings from one cell line, the genomic abnormalities that we identified in 2XSB cells may also point to some of the signaling pathways that will be important therapeutic targets in neurofibromin-positive sporadic MPNSTs.

Potential Layer 4 — Actual Data Content

Horizon

VCF Files → Patient Variants

Per-patient variant call files transformed into graph nodes, linking patients to specific genomic variants, allele frequencies, and affected genes — enabling population-level queries across the cohort.

  • Query "which patients share this splice variant?"
  • Cross-link to cell lines and animal models carrying the same variant
  • Aggregate variant burden across studies

Images → Embedded Representations

Histology, MRI, and microscopy images encoded as vector embeddings and stored as graph node properties, enabling similarity-based retrieval alongside structured metadata.

  • Find tumors with similar morphology
  • Link image findings to genomic or clinical context
  • Multimodal queries combining text and image

Currently out of scope and would need governance review

Potential Layer 5 — User Knowledge & Hypotheses

Horizon

What if researchers could annotate hypotheses and in-progress findings directly onto each data file in Synapse? Those annotations could flow into the KG as a queryable, creditable layer of researcher knowledge.

  • Pre-publication findings become visible and queryable across the community
  • Hypothesis nodes link to specific files, datasets, or tool records already in the KG
  • Negative results and open questions can be surfaced, not buried in notebooks

The Approach

Spotify built a graph over queries, songs, artists, genres, and topics, then applied node2vec to learn vector representations of all entities. These embeddings power real-time query suggestions in instant search.

  • Graph captures co-occurrence and behavioral relationships
  • Suggestions are diverse, not just semantically similar
  • Outperformed transformer-based semantic similarity baseline

Graph Learning on the NF KG

Horizon

Beyond Retrieval

The current KG supports SPARQL queries and RAG retrieval. Graph learning can go further — embedding the graph structure itself to power richer discovery.

  • Node embeddings — entities in vector space from graph neighborhood
  • Link prediction — surface likely but unrecorded relationships
  • Query suggestions — recommend related exploratory queries
  • Anomaly detection — flag unexpected connections or data gaps

Applying it for NF

  • Sparse, heterogeneous data across tools, publications, and ontologies
  • Researchers often don't know what to ask — exploratory queries dominate
  • Cross-modal connections hard to express in SQL/SPARQL
  • Small community benefits from surfacing non-obvious relationships
  • No search query logs linked to entities yet — node2vec applies to the KG graph structure directly; query-entity associations could be bootstrapped from interaction data over time

Supporting SageBrain

  • The NF KG pipeline outlines adoptable practices for building domain-specific knowledge graphs on top of Synapse data — schema design, ontology mapping, RDF transformation, and SPARQL-based retrieval
  • Serves as a reference implementation that other Sage projects can adapt, with documented tooling (Dagster, RML, QLever) and reproducible build processes
  • Efforts underway to load the RDF dataset into Amazon Neptune, enabling managed graph infrastructure and integration with AWS-native services
  • Will be providing support to the EL project, applying lessons from the NF pipeline to help structure and query their knowledge graph
